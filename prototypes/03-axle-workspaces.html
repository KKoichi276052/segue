Prototype 0.3 · workspaces, windows and apps in one ring

Segue

A workspace is a saved window setup, such as an editor on the left half and a browser on the right. A ring of workspaces hugs the notch. Turn it to see each one full size, then press return to bring it back. Press ▼ to pick a single window, snap it to half the screen, or send it to another workspace. Everything shown is simulated.

Keys

⌥ space
Open the workspace ring. Plain space works here too.
◀ ▶ · 1–6
Turn the ring to see another workspace, or jump to one by number. Clicking a side glance turns the ring too.
return
Bring that workspace back: open missing apps and put every window in its place.
▼ then ◀ ▶
Pick one window in the preview. return focuses it. ▲ goes back to the ring.
⇧ + arrows
Snap the picked window: left half, right half, full, centered.
⇧ + 1–6 · ⇧ delete
Send the window to workspace 1–6 · close it.
⌥ hold + tab
Recent windows across all workspaces. Let go to switch. ⌥ ` cycles only the current app's windows.
Any letter
Search every window title and workspace name.

Switch log

    What to judge

    • Biggest or glances. Switch between the three preview options while turning the ring. Thin glances keep the preview at full size. Wide glances shrink it by about a sixth.
    • Peeking islands. The two islands at the ends of the ring sit half behind the top edge, blurred. Is that enough of a hint that more workspaces are there?
    • Instead of Mission Control. Turn the ring with ◀ ▶. Is seeing each workspace full size, without hovering, the thing you were missing?
    • Front or hide. Open "Design review", then "Comms", once in each mode. The first is what the plan covers early. The second is the full window-manager version you said is best eventually.
    • Sending a window. Pick a window, press ⇧ 2, and watch it fly into that island. Is it obvious where it went?
    • Arranging. Are halves, full and centered enough, or do you need thirds and quarters?
    • Finding. Hold ⌥ and tap tab. The ring turns to whichever workspace holds the window. Does that answer "where is it?" faster than searching?

    "Weekly sync" holds apps that aren't open yet, so bringing it back launches them. Windows that belong to no workspace live on the dotted island. Thumbnails are drawn placeholders. The real app would capture window images with Screen Recording access and arrange windows through Accessibility.
